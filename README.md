## Year 1

<details>
<summary><b>Semester 1</b></summary>

<br>

<details>
<summary>Operating systems for parallel and distributed architectures</summary>

> Hands-on course focused on parallel cluster systems - from hardware and software architecture to building, installing, configuring, maintaining, and troubleshooting clusters.

<details>
<summary>Formal models of concurrent processes</summary>

> Covers serializability, concurrency control algorithms, and parallel programming paradigms. Progresses into multiversion data, distributed concurrency, error recovery, and formal methods including process algebra, CCS, and Pi-calculus.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Statistical computational methods</summary>

> Covers random variables, discrete and continuous simulation methods, Markov chains, Poisson and binomial counting processes, queueing systems, and statistical inference.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Cloud databases</summary>

> Explores database systems designed for cloud environments, covering topics such as distributed storage, data replication, consistency models, and scalability strategies.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Academic ethics and integrity. Methodology of scientific research</summary>

> Explores ethical frameworks, plagiarism, data fabrication, authorship ethics, peer review bias, and whistleblowing through real-world case studies. Also covers AI ethics, open science, and human/animal research ethics.
</details>
